ICASSP 2027 SP GRAND CHALLENGES · GC-15

From understanding audio
to knowing when to respond.

HEARTBEAT Challenge 2027 brings together real-time speech, audio-language, multimodal, and embodied AI research around a central question: whether, when, and how an agent should respond.

A hesitation should not trigger an early reply. A correction may require the assistant to stop speaking. An event in the incoming audio or video may call for a timely alert. HEARTBEAT tests these decisions as input arrives, measuring what agents do and when they do it.

01 / TRACKS

Two tracks. One interaction challenge.

TRACK I

Reciprocal Audio Turn-Taking

Decide when to speak, keep listening, continue speaking, or yield. Distinguish pauses, backchannels, and side speech from genuine interruptions.

Offline
Given an audio prefix and assistant instructions, select WAIT, RESPOND, CONTINUE, or YIELD. Submit one selected action per sample as JSON.
Online
Process audio as it arrives and generate speech. Submit output audio and timestamped JSON interaction logs.

Offline and Online are evaluated and ranked separately.

TRACK II

Streaming Proactive Understanding

Follow a user instruction, identify relevant events as the stream unfolds, and respond when needed. Remain silent otherwise.

Audio-Visual
Use video and audio to understand the ongoing stream.
Audio-Only
Use audio alone to understand the ongoing stream.

Both subtracks cover Perception, Comprehension, and Reasoning, with Online evaluation and separate rankings.

Training and development data

03 / EVALUATION

The right response, at the right time.

Track I · Offline

Top-1 Accuracy

Incorrect, missing, or unparseable predictions count as errors.

Track I · Online

Behavior, quality & timing

Evaluate turn-taking decisions, response quality, and the timing of speech onset and offset.

Track II · Online

Precision, Recall & joint F1

Responses must be timely and correct. Missed events, false activations, and duplicate responses reduce the score.

Online protocol

Use only current and previously received input. For Track II, log every chunk, including chunks with no response. Timestamp each response at the end of the chunk that produces it; timestamps cannot be backdated.
